AUTHOR:Robson
TITLE:I don’t know

Watching about feelings
Feeling like how it would feel
Compensation is the word
That repeats endlessly
But I think nothing

It’s like a havoc is happening
Deep inside my fragile mind
It thinks so highly of it
On top of anything
Diverging of others selves

How meaningful are words
That are heard without listening?
How good am I
To see the world around?

In the end, it all results in nothing
Nothing is categorized though
It grows like dark matter, expanding, twisting space
Invading, turning, creeping, disappearing
And so, hollow be I
